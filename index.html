Enter a destination to create a short link. Customize its name and challenge, or leave optional fields blank.

DISCLAIMER

Photosensitivity warning: the challenge uses flashing, blinking, and rotating visuals plus audio, and may not be suitable for people with photosensitive epilepsy or motion sensitivity. Only HTTPS destinations are accepted. Link names are identifiers, not encryption, and anyone with a link can use it. The destination is stored on the site database, so links work only while this website and its database are available. Do not use this for passwords, private documents, or other sensitive information. Some destination sites may block redirects, and the challenge requires JavaScript and a successful database lookup; audio may be blocked by browser settings. Does not support emojis or special characters in the link name, title, nor button text. The site is not responsible for any content on destination sites.
